ros2 launch mujoco_ros2 example.py

ros2 topic pub /joint_commands std_msgs/msg/Float64MultiArray "{data: [-1000.0, 100.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0]}"

ros2 topic pub --once /joint_commands std_msgs/msg/Float64MultiArray "{data: [-1000.0, 100.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0]}"
